Architecture health, ten views

A second set built to answer one question at a glance: which modules are core, and which have problems with separation of concerns (MVC), interfaces, duplication, locks, organization, complexity or coupling. All ten share one product design and one real run of Archify analyzing itself. Each view gets three frames, and the third is the dark theme. Click a frame to enlarge it; the arrow keys move through all thirty.

Captured at 1440 × 900 (2× pixels) from the pages in experiments/atlas-showcase/pro. Complexity and coupling come from analyzer findings; the other five areas are heuristic signals with file:line evidence (see “How it is measured” on any page).
