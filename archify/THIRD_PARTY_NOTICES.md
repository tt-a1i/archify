## Full-colour logos (gilbarbara/logos and devicon)

Architecture node tiles can show a full-colour logo for a brand. These logos
are fetched and sanitized by `scripts/generate-brand-logos.mjs` from two pinned
sources, and each embedded logo records its provider, license, repository,
commit, file, and SHA-256 in `renderers/shared/generated-brand-logos.mjs`
(manifest: `brand-marks/logos.json`). Only the logos a diagram uses are
embedded in its output, and their colours are not changed.

- [gilbarbara/logos](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/tree/d6960edc1b7309d392c76e40d3a320df7f9ff85c)
  at commit `d6960edc1b7309d392c76e40d3a320df7f9ff85c`, dedicated to the public
  domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/blob/d6960edc1b7309d392c76e40d3a320df7f9ff85c/LICENSE.txt).
- [devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/32fe1d3b3f36ad11f90ab94f7db59738139c2bf6)
  at commit `32fe1d3b3f36ad11f90ab94f7db59738139c2bf6`, Copyright (c) 2015 konpa,
  distributed under the MIT License. The complete license text is in
  `third_party/devicon/LICENSE` in the packaged Skill.

When neither source has a logo, the Simple Icons mark above is used.
Logos are trademarks of their respective owners.

## JetBrains Mono

Delivered Archify viewer artifacts embed the JetBrains Mono variable font
subsets served by Google Fonts. For characters covered by these subsets, font
selection does not depend on a network request or a locally installed copy.
Uncovered characters (including CJK) still use the system fallback stack;
browser and operating-system rasterization can differ.
JetBrains Mono is maintained at
[github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono)
and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete license
text is preserved in `assets/JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt` in the packaged Skill and
in the font CSS carried by standalone HTML and SVG exports.

## Pele

The explainer renders Mermaid chart fences with an unmodified subset of
[Pele 1.3.0](https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele), copyright (c) 2026 kepano,
distributed under the MIT License. The subset and its license text are in
