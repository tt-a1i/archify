# Third-party brand assets

Archify's MIT license covers Archify's code. It does **not** license third-party
brand names, logos, trademarks, or artwork bundled for referential diagrams.
Those assets remain the property of their respective owners. Their presence does
not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or that every use is permitted.

The machine-readable policy is `brand-marks/rights.json`. `HOLD` assets are not
bundled or renderable. `COUNSEL` means Archify has not confirmed a general right
to redistribute the asset; users must assess their own use. `KEEP_WITH_NOTICE`
means relevant source, attribution, license, or brand conditions exist and still
do not amount to an Archify sublicense.
